Postdoctoral Scholar in Biology and Biological Engineering at Caltech

Studying molecular circuits and signaling specificity in biological systems.

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Elowitz Lab at the California Institute of Technology. My current research focuses on synthetic biology and cellular signaling dynamics. I completed my PhD in Chemistry at the University of Washington in the Zalatan Lab, where I studied how scaffold proteins like Axin promote signaling specificity in the Wnt pathway. Prior to my graduate studies, I received my BS in Chemistry from Rice University.

Research Interests

Signaling Specificity

Investigating how cells distinguish between competing signals to execute precise biological programs, particularly through scaffold proteins.

Synthetic Biology

Engineering new molecular circuits to understand and control cellular behaviors in complex environments.
